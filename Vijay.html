vijay

Vijay is an Indian actor and playback singer who works in Tamil cinema. He is one of the highest-paid actors in Tamil cinema and has a huge fan following. Vijay made his acting debut as a child artist in the film "Vetri" (1984) and later appeared in several films as a lead actor. Some of his notable films include "Ghilli," "Thuppakki," "Mersal," and "Master." He has received numerous awards for his performances, including Tamil Nadu State Film Awards and Filmfare Awards South.
